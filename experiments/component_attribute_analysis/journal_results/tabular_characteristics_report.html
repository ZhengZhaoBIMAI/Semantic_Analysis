两个面板如何理解？

A — Attribute occupancy：每条表示相应固定列表格中非空单元格的比例。全局按 1,968 个字段建表，各构件类别使用本类别的字段并集；全局非空比例为 1.01%。空白可能是不适用或未提供，不等于数据错误。

B — Attribute-count distribution：每个箱体汇总相应类别全部构件的非空属性数量。箱体为中央 50%，中线为中位数；须采用标准 1.5 倍四分位距规则，须外点表示实际观测，不直接代表错误。横轴为线性坐标，保留全部观测。

英文图注

Figure 1. Structural sparsity and attribute-count variability of BIM object semantics. The analysis includes 92,338 graph objects from 214 models after excluding model_6; attribute vocabularies are recomputed from the retained models. (A) Percentage of non-empty cells in a fixed-column representation. The All objects row uses the global union of 1,968 property paths; each class-specific row uses the property-path union observed within that class. Occupancy equals the observed non-empty key-value occurrences divided by the number of objects multiplied by the corresponding vocabulary size. Structural blanks can reflect inapplicable or unprovided attributes. (B) Object-level distributions of non-empty attribute counts on a linear scale. Boxes indicate the interquartile range; central lines indicate medians. Whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges from each box. Points indicate observations beyond the whiskers; overlapping points may coincide and do not denote data errors. Zero and false are counted as non-empty. The figure describes observed full property paths before comprehensive synonym harmonization; it is not a semantic-quality or model-performance assessment.
